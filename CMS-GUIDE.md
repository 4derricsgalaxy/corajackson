# Editing the family site in Wix CMS

Everything on the site comes from five collections in the Wix dashboard (Site → CMS). Changes show up on the site within an hour, or within seconds if the revalidation automation is set up.

## Add a new family member

1. Open **Family Members** → **Add item**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Full Name** (required) and **Slug** (required, lowercase, no spaces, e.g. `maya-jackson`). The slug becomes the URL `/family/maya-jackson`.
   - **Parent**: pick the mother or father from the list. This is what places them on the tree.
   - **Family Line**: pick which of Cora's eight children this person descends from (the site also works this out, but setting it keeps albums tidy).
   - **Generation**: 1 for Cora's children, 2 for grandchildren, 3 for great-grandchildren, 4 for great-great-grandchildren.
   - **Portrait**: upload or choose from the Media Manager. A vertical (4:5) photo looks best.
   - **Short Bio**: one or two sentences shown on cards and in the tree panel.
   - **Biography**: the full story (rich text).
   - **Other Parent / Family Note**: e.g. `Mother: Jane Doe (married 2010)`.
   - **Published**: leave on. Turn off to hide someone without deleting them.
3. Save. A new leaf appears on the tree and a new page is created automatically.

## Add photos

1. Open **Photos** → **Add item**.
2. Upload the **Image**, give it a **Title**, optional **Caption** and **Year Taken**.
3. **People Pictured**: tag everyone in the photo. Tagged photos appear on each person's page.
4. **Family Line**: choose the line the photo belongs to. This decides which album it's filed in on `/gallery`.
5. **Featured in Gallery**: turn on for the best photos; they appear on the home page.

You can also add several photos at once to a person's **Photo Gallery** field on their Family Members item.

## Stories and history

- **Stories**: title, slug, story text, cover image, "Told By", and tag the people in it.
- **History Timeline**: title, year, a date label like "Spring 1952", text, image, location, people.

## Site settings

The **Site Settings** collection has one item: site title, tagline, the home page intro paragraph, the hero portrait of Cora, the tree artwork, and the footer text.
